# v3.1.4 - Remote Pre-Trade Risk Evaluation

Order submission now persists a `risk_pending` order before calling the Risk Engine. The remote call is made outside the database transaction. A second transaction then locks the order, persists the final pre-trade decision, and either accepts/matches the order or moves it to `risk_rejected`.

Default behavior is fail closed. If the Risk Engine is unavailable, times out, or returns an invalid response, the order does not enter the active book and no executions are created. Development fail-open is available through `PRE_TRADE_RISK_FAIL_OPEN=true`; bypass decisions are persisted with `RISK_SERVICE_BYPASSED`.

Risk limits are loaded from `pre_trade_risk_policies`, with tenant-specific policy taking precedence over the platform default. Daily limits apply to approved order notional for the configured policy risk day. The initial seed policy uses UTC daily reset.

Estimated price policy:

- `LIMIT` and `STOP_LIMIT` use `limitPrice`.
- `MARKET`, `STOP`, and `STOP_LOSS` fail closed with `REFERENCE_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` until a reliable reference-price source is wired into the synchronous path.

The release emits `order.risk_rejected` for business denials and `order.risk_error` for fail-closed dependency/response failures through the existing transactional outbox. Approval reuses existing `order.accepted`, fill, and trade execution events.
